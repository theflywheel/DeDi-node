Replication — a different thing entirely

Copies of one node, run by one operator, sharing one key and one log. They buy uptime. They prove nothing about each other, which is why they are drawn inside a boundary rather than joined by arrows: reading “three replicas agree” as “three parties verified” is precisely the wrong conclusion.
